# OER-Stellar
## Purpose: Complete OER Textbook.
## Goals
- Complete editing an OER Stellar Astronomy textbook so that it is ready for publication.
- Streamline the editing process using AI tools.
- Carefully track AI usage and content and separate from human work.
  - Design tools primarily for formatting text and completing repetitive tasks. Minimize generating text.
  - When text is generated by AI, make sure that it is reviewed by a human.
  - Have AI suggest changes based on grammar, spelling, accessibility, and IDEA - inclusivity, diversity, equity, and anti-racism based on standards supplied by user.
## Constraints
- Single human user
- Large amount of material
- Short deadline - August 31
- Accessibility challenges - astronomy uses a lot of visuals
## Decisions
- Switch to AI - majority of tasks are repetitive or formatting, want user to focus on content matter specialty.
