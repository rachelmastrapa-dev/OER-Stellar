# Style for Exercise Box

If present, maintain class attributes similar to `class="lt-phys-64799"`

# HTML Format

<section class="box-exercise">
<h5><span class="lt-icon-default">Exercise Name</span></h5>

<p>Exercise text</p>

<dl>
    <dt><strong class="emphasis bold">Answer</strong></dt>
    <dd>
    <p>Equation</p>
    </dd>
</dl>
</section>

# Example

<section class="box-exercise">
<h5 class="box-legend lt-phys-64799"><span class="lt-icon-default">Exercise Gravity on a 2M<sub>Sun</sub> White Dwarf</span></h5>

<p class="lt-phys-64799">What is the acceleration of gravity at the surface if the white dwarf has the twice the mass of the Sun and is only half the radius of Earth?</p>

<dl>
    <dt><strong class="emphasis bold">Answer</strong></dt>
    <dd>
    <p class="lt-phys-64799">\[g(\text{white dwarf}) = \frac{(G \times 2M_{\text{Sun}})}{(0.5R_{\text{Earth}})^{2}} = \frac{(6.67 \times 10^{-11} \text{ m}^2/\text{kg s}^2 \times 4 \times 10^{30} \text{ kg})}{(3.2 \times 10^{6})^2} = 2.61 \times 10^{7} \text{ m/s}^2 \nonumber\]</p>
    </dd>
</dl>
</section>
