This first section of the chapter is devoted to exploring and understanding how ancient astronomers used the sky for navigation and everyday life. 

Many learners of Astronomy think of the first astronomers as the Greeks or Babylonians. This section is devoted to shifting that narrative to consider the long, long history of humanity and astronomy practices that expand far beyond these widely shared historical perspectives. The paragraphs that follow are by no means a complete account of the astronomy knowledge that has been developed around the world. Instead, the examples below are meant to explore a more global perspective on historical astronomy knowledge, and how this knowledge has been preserved to today.
