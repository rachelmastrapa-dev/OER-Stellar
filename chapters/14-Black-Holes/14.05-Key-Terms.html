Chapter Glossary

  • escape velocity: The minimum speed that must be achieved for a free, non-propelled object to escape from the gravitational influence of a massive body, i.e. to achieve an infinite distance from it.
  • event horizon: The distance from a black hole where the escape velocity equals the speed of light, the fastest possible speed known, and within which nothing can escape. In this sense, the event horizon is a "point of no return." See also: Schwarzschild radius.
  • gravitational wave: A disturbance in the curvature of spacetime caused by changes in how matter is distributed; gravitational waves propagate at (or near) the speed of light.
  • singularity: The point of zero volume and infinite density to which any object that becomes a black hole must collapse, according to the theory of general relativity.
