Introduction

To learn more about the stars, astronomers need to collect information about them, and find out how many there are of each type of star. Putting together this kind of information can lead to a better understanding of how stars form and change over time.

Learning Objectives
  • Explain how an astronomer can measure the temperature of stars based on their colors
  • Describe how astronomers derive the sum of star masses in a binary star system
  • Describe the relationship between stellar mass and stellar luminosity
  • Describe the methods used to determine star diameters
  • Identify the parts of an eclipsing binary star light curve that correspond to the diameters of the individual components
  • Describe the relationship between stellar temperature and luminosity
  • Identify the key components of an H-R diagram, including the values and meaning of the axes and different groups of stars
  • Describe the physical properties of the different groups of stars on the H-R diagram, such as radius, and for main sequence stars, mass
