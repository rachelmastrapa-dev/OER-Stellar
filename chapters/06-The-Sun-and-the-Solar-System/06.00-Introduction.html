Introduction

The Sun is the closest star in the sky. Not only does it keep us alive, but it gives astronomers an opportunity to learn about stars. Let's take a look at the layers of the Sun and how the Sun affects the Solar System.

Learning Objectives
  • Explain how the composition of the Sun differs from that of Earth
  • Describe the various layers of the Sun and their functions
  • Describe the sunspot cycle and the solar cycle
  • Explain how magnetism is the source of solar activity
  • Explain what space weather is and how it affects Earth
  • Explain the importance of collisions in the formation of the Solar System
